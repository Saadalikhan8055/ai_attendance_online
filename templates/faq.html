Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about AI Attendance

What is AI Attendance?

AI Attendance is an automated face recognition system designed to streamline attendance marking in educational institutions and organizations. It uses facial recognition technology to quickly and accurately mark attendance without manual processes.

How accurate is the face detection?

Our system achieves 99% accuracy in facial recognition under optimal conditions. Accuracy depends on photo quality, lighting, and consistent appearance. We recommend clear, front-facing photos during enrollment.

Is the system secure and private?

Yes! We use encrypted data storage, secure authentication, and comply with privacy regulations. Face data is stored securely and never shared with third parties. All communications are encrypted.

What are the hardware requirements?
  • Webcam: Any standard USB webcam or built-in camera
  • Processor: Intel i5 or equivalent
  • RAM: 2GB minimum, 4GB recommended
  • Internet: Stable connection for real-time processing
Does it work offline?

The system requires internet connectivity for enrollment and data synchronization. However, captured attendance data is cached locally. We recommend maintaining a stable connection for optimal performance.

What browsers are supported?
  • Google Chrome (v90+)
  • Mozilla Firefox (v88+)
  • Safari (v14+)
  • Microsoft Edge (v90+)
I forgot my password, what should I do?

Contact your system administrator to reset your password. For security reasons, passwords cannot be reset through the system directly. The admin can issue a temporary password.

How do I export attendance data?

Go to Dashboard → Reports → Export Data. This will download your attendance records as a CSV file that can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets.
