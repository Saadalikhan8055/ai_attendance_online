Getting Started

Welcome to AI Attendance! This guide will help you get up and running quickly.

Prerequisites:

  • Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • Webcam for face enrollment and capture
  • Stable internet connection
  • Admin account credentials

User Registration

Step 1: Visit the login page and click "Create Account"

Step 2: Enter your username and password

Step 3: Click "Register" to create your account

Step 4: Use your credentials to login

Tip: Use a strong password with at least 8 characters for security.

Student Enrollment

Step 1: Go to Dashboard → Register Student

Step 2: Enter student details (Name, Student ID, Email)

Step 3: Drag and drop a clear photo or click to upload

Step 4: The system will process the face data automatically

Step 5: Click "Register" to save the student

Best Practices:
  • Use clear, front-facing photos
  • Ensure good lighting in photo
  • Face should occupy 50-70% of image
  • No glasses or excessive makeup

Face Capture & Detection

Step 1: Go to Dashboard → Start Capture

Step 2: Position yourself in front of the webcam

Step 3: The system will detect your face automatically

Step 4: Wait for the "Attendance Marked" confirmation

Step 5: Check the detection log for results

Success: Green indicator means face was successfully recognized.

Marking Attendance

Attendance is marked automatically when a face is recognized during capture session.

Real-time Detection Log:

  • Shows live feed from webcam
  • Updates detection results every 2 seconds
  • Displays recognized student names
  • Shows timestamps for each detection

Reports & Analytics

Access Reports:

  • Analytics: Dashboard with KPIs and trends
  • Reports: Generate filtered attendance reports
  • Export: Download data as CSV files

Available Metrics:

  • Total enrollment
  • Average attendance percentage
  • At-risk students (low attendance)
  • Daily attendance breakdown
  • Student-wise attendance history

Troubleshooting

Face not being detected?

✓ Check lighting - ensure your face is well lit

✓ Move closer to webcam

✓ Remove glasses or obstructions

✓ Ensure webcam is clean and working

Wrong attendance record shown?

✓ System may need face re-enrollment

✓ Check student photo quality

✓ Update enrollment with newer photo

Webcam permission error?

✓ Allow camera access in browser settings

✓ Check browser permissions

✓ Try different browser if issue persists
